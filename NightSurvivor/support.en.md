# Support

## Eternal Night Hunter: Survivor
A roguelite survival game where you fight off endless hordes of monsters to survive the night.
Level up, choose weapons and passives, and take on the true bosses of the Eternal Night, Ruins, Labyrinth, and Turning Tide stages.

## Requirements
- iPhone / iPad with iOS 18.6 or later
- Landscape only

## FAQ

### Ads are not showing
An internet connection is required to show ads. Please try again where your connection is stable.
It may take a few seconds for ads to become ready right after launch.

### I watched an ad but did not get the reward
Rewards are only granted if you watch the ad to the end.

### I don't want to see ads
You can turn ads off from Settings on the title screen.
When ads are off, the ad buttons are hidden and you receive bonuses such as extra gold and rerolls instead.

### Can I transfer my data?
Game data is stored only on your device. Transferring data to another device is not supported.
Please note that deleting the app also deletes your data.

### I want to start over
Use "Reset Data" in Settings on the title screen to erase your records, achievements, gold, and upgrades.
"Reset Upgrades" sets all upgrades back to 0 and refunds the gold you spent.
