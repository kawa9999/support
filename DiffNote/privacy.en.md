# Privacy Policy

This app A note app that puts two texts side by side and shows you what changed, at a glance.

[Where Your Notes Are Stored]
* On your device: Your notes, folders, and pin and lock states are stored on your device.
* iCloud (optional): Only if you turn on iCloud in Settings, your notes are stored in your own private area of Apple's iCloud (CloudKit) and sync across your iPhone, iPad, and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account. iCloud is off by default. Apple manages this data, and the developer has no access to it. iCloud is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

[Deleting Your Data]
* You can delete notes in the App. Notes permanently deleted from the trash cannot be recovered.
* Deleting the App removes the data stored on your device.
* You can manage or delete iCloud data under iCloud in your device's Settings app.

[Information Collected]
This app does not collect user personal information (such as name or email address).

[About Advertising]
This application does not display advertisements.

[Provision to Third Parties]
Collected information will not be provided to third parties.

For inquiries, please contact us via the support URL listed on the App Store.

This policy is subject to change without notice.
